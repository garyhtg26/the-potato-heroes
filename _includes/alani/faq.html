FAQs

Here's a little help section — and if you need more, we're only one click away.

Do I need a reservation?

For the restaurant and bar, we recommend making a reservation — we get busy during peak hours. The bakery is walk-in friendly.

Can I visit all three in one day?

Absolutely. Many guests start with coffee and pastries, stay for lunch or dinner, then end the day at the bar — all in one visit.

Do you offer takeaway or delivery?

Yes, selected items are available for takeaway, and we work with delivery partners for orders within the area.

Are you family-friendly?

Yes. We welcome families, and we’re happy to accommodate little ones — just let us know if you need extra space or a high chair.
