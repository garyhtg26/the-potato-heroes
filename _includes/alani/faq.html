FAQs

Here's a little help section — and if you need more, we're only one click away.

What are your operating hours?

We're open Thursday to Sunday from 9:30 AM to 3 PM. Perfect for a slow morning or a cosy brunch.

What types of bread & pastries do you offer?

We focus on Greek and Mediterranean flavours inspired by Chef Alberto’s Greek and Turkish grandmothers — a “Nomadic Dough” full of nostalgia and comfort.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes, we do! We offer gluten-free and vegan-friendly options. However, we work with a lot of gluten in our kitchen, so guests with celiac disease should take extra care and speak to our team.

Do you provide pick-up and delivery?

We're working on it! Keep an eye on our updates — we want to make it as easy as possible for you to enjoy Alani at home.

Are walk-ins allowed, or is it reservation-only?

Walk-ins are always welcome. For a smoother experience and shorter waiting time, we still recommend making a reservation.

Do you accept private bookings for 12 pax and above?

Yes, we do. For events and special occasions of 12 guests or more, email us at reservations@thepotatoheroes.com and we’ll help you plan it.

Is the outdoor space covered?

Yes. Our outdoor area is covered and equipped with fans, so you can enjoy the breeze while staying comfortable.

Are pets allowed?

Absolutely! We’re pet-friendly — bring your furry friends along for a relaxed day out.

Where is Alani located?

We’re on the 4th floor of the newly renovated KADA building in the CBD. The nearest MRT stations are Maxwell, Tanjong Pagar, and Chinatown.
